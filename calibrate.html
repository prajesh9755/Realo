Why this exists

Phones estimate where your floor is, and that estimate is not perfect. On some devices every object lands a few centimetres too tall. Measuring a known cube once lets us correct for it on this phone, in this room.

  1. Tap Place the 1 m cube and set it on the floor.
  2. Hold a tape measure against one vertical edge, on the real floor.
  3. Type what the tape actually reads.
